First Light

A Finnish lakeside pin game where every throw changes the field—in four dimensions.

Knock down one pin to score its number, or several to score their count. Reach exactly 50. Pins stay where they land, including in neighbouring W slices. Follow their blue and amber shadows to plan your next throw.

Download and launch

  1. Download the ZIP, then extract it with Archive Utility.
  2. Open the macos folder and launch FirstLight4D.app.
  3. Choose Start solo match. No Unity editor, headset, phone or internet connection is needed to play.

Validation configuration: Apple M4 MacBook Air, 16 GB RAM, macOS 26.3, Metal, keyboard/mouse, fixed 1280×720 window. This is a test configuration, not a measured minimum. Windows, VR and phone controls are not part of this package.

Your first match

  • Mouse aims. Hold Space to charge; release once to throw. T makes a quick throw.
  • After the score appears, press T for the next turn. Fallen pins stand where they landed.
  • Blue shadows mean −W; amber means +W. F follows a target into its slice without auto-aim; you aim the shot yourself. Q/E moves through W, and 0 returns home.
  • Exactly 50 wins. Overshoot and return to 25. Three consecutive misses ends the match.
  • Esc pauses, H shows help, V inspects, R restarts. After pause or target selection, release Space and T before pressing again.

About this build

0.11.1 removes automatic aiming from pin selection. Single-pin shots now depend on your mouse aim; selecting a number only changes W. The game retains its fixed-window IL2CPP profile, wider camera, persistent W shadows, ground-led falls, twelve-pin scoring and input-transition safety. There is no random spread added. The solver is deterministic game physics, not a general-purpose 4D rigid-body simulator.

Game concept and direction by Ilkka Lappeteläinen. Built with Unity and HackerPoet’s MIT-licensed Engine4D, with AI coding assistance. No live generative AI is needed to play. Mölkky is the rules reference, not a brand affiliation. Credits and provenance.
