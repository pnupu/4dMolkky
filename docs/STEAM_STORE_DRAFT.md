## Short description

A tactical wooden-pin game in four dimensions. Aim, throw, and reach exactly 50. Follow fallen pins into neighbouring slices of space, choose your next target, and try to beat your best solo match.

## About this game

Twelve wooden pins. A throwing skittle. A quiet field by the lake.

One fallen pin scores its number. Several score their count. You need exactly 50: go over and you return to 25; miss three times in a row and the match ends.

The fourth dimension changes what you can see and hit. Pins can leave your current slice without leaving the field. Move through W to find them, or focus a numbered target to line up your next throw. Optional outlines show neighbouring slices without replacing the solid cross-section.

- Play complete solo matches and track your best winning throw count locally.
- Aim with a mouse and charge throws with the keyboard.
- Re-stand pins at their landing positions, including their W displacement.
- Switch between close aiming and a wider field view.
- Adjust sound and mouse sensitivity; pause or restart at any time.

The current game uses a deterministic four-dimensional gameplay solver. It is not a general-purpose rigid-body simulation.
