# Steam Release Requirements

Steam publication requires external approval and rights clearance in addition to a working game.

## Key points

- Valve requires store and build review; each normally takes 3–5 business days, with at least seven days recommended for submissions.
- A new product needs a publicly visible Coming Soon page for at least two weeks. Onboarding also describes a 30-day app-fee waiting period for the first few releases.
- Store claims must describe implemented features and supported operating systems that launch successfully.
- The publisher must have adequate rights to the content. Mölkky's official website identifies Tactic Games Oy as its brand owner; this repository has no verified permission record.
- Unity supplies an official patcher and patched editor versions for CVE-2025-59489. Release binaries must be checked and, where applicable, patched and retested.
- Valve's content survey must be completed before review, including an accurate account of any AI-assisted player-consumed content. The current documentation distinguishes this from development-tool efficiency alone.
- Store submission needs at least five actual gameplay screenshots at 1920×1080 or greater, plus store/library artwork. Gameplay screenshots must not be marketing composites. Branded capsule artwork remains dependent on the commercial naming decision.

## Project uncertainty
