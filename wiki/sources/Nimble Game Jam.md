# Nimble Game Jam

The Nimble Game Jam is the fixed 48-hour production window for the first jam version of 4D Mölkky.

## Key Facts

- The jam runs from Friday 4 September 2026 at 18:00 to Sunday 6 September 2026 at 18:00 in Helsinki.
- It is hybrid: remote participation is allowed, while the Helsinki venue at Founders House requires a separate, capacity-limited registration.
- Participation requires joining both the itch.io jam and the Nimble Fox Discord.
- Teams can have up to four people.
- The theme is revealed at the opening ceremony, so theme-specific implementation must wait until the jam begins.
- Any engine, tools, and AI assistance are allowed.
- Premade or purchased assets are allowed when their creators are credited.
- The submitted game must be made during the jam window and include clear download instructions and system specifications.
- The deadline is strict. A playable build and itch.io page should be uploaded well before Sunday at 18:00.

## Project Implications

- Before the jam, prepare tooling, hardware, reusable public assets, documentation templates, and technical experiments—not the final themed game.
- Optimize for a small downloadable desktop build unless standalone headset deployment is proven before Friday.
- VR should be a validated enhancement or separate build, not an untested requirement that can prevent submission.
- Keep an asset-credit ledger from the start.
